# 4x4 Cube Simulator

# Description

This is a 4x4 Cube simulator which just lets you click and drag on the cube to rotate it, has buttons to turn it, including wide moves, and all the other face moves, and also has a scramble function.

## How To Use It

You just click and drag on the cube to rotate it, click buttons to do your turns, (wide means 2 layers), and you can scramble it.

## How I Made It

After I made the 3x3, some friends asked me how I did it. I would print out a paper with the unfolded 3x3 on it, number each sticker, and then cut it up and fold it into a cube. Then, I used the numbers to make pairs of points for each turn. The same goes for the 4x4.
